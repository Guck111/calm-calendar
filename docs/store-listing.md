## English (en-us)

**Product name:** Calm Calendar

**Short title:** Calm Calendar

**Short description** (shown in some places instead of the full description):

> A calm, ad-free calendar for Windows. Your Google Calendar in day, week and month views, with a desktop widget, quick add and reminders.

**Description:**

> Calm Calendar is a quiet, fast and ad-free calendar for Windows that works with your Google Calendar.
>
> See your schedule in day, week and month views, drag events to move or resize them, and keep a desktop widget with a clock, a countdown to your next event, a mini month and your upcoming events.
>
> Add events by typing them in plain words: "tomorrow 7pm gym 50 min #personal" creates the event in the right calendar at the right time. Your Google Calendar reminders arrive as Windows notifications, and when you are offline the app still shows the schedule it loaded last.
>
> Private by design: Calm Calendar has no servers of its own, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your calendar data goes only between your PC and Google.
>
> Requires a Google account. The interface is available in English and Russian.

**Product features** (one per line, up to 20):

- Day, week and month views of your Google Calendar
- Drag events to move them or change their length
- Desktop widget with a clock, countdown, mini month and upcoming events
- Quick add: type "tomorrow 7pm gym 50 min" and press Enter
- Google Calendar reminders as Windows notifications
- Works offline with the last loaded schedule
- Show or hide each of your calendars
- Search across your events
- Light, dark and system themes with a glass effect
- Launch at sign-in with just the widget
- No ads, no tracking, no servers of ours
- English and Russian interface
