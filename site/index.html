Calm Calendar

A free, ad-free desktop calendar for Windows that shows your Google Calendar in day, week, month and schedule views, with a desktop widget and quick add from any app.

Everything you need, nothing you don't

A native Windows window for your Google Calendar. No ads, no feeds, no clutter.

  • Day, week, month and schedule

    Your Google Calendar in a fast native window. Drag events to move or resize them; repeats, guests and time zones included.

  • Desktop widget

    Clock, a countdown to your next event, a mini month and upcoming events right on the desktop.

  • Quick add from any app

    Press Ctrl+Alt+Space anywhere, type “tomorrow 7pm gym 50 min” and press Enter. Understands all 13 languages of the app.

  • Never miss a call

    A Join button for Google Meet, Zoom and Teams, and an optional alert over all windows when a meeting starts.

  • Several accounts

    Work and personal Google accounts in one window, with clashes between them marked. Add class schedules or Outlook calendars by their .ics link.

  • Fast from the keyboard

    Ctrl+K for any command, arrows to move between events, E to edit, Ctrl+D to duplicate. Press ? for the full list.

  • Five styles, light and dark

    Classic, Paper, Obsidian, Showcase or Monochrome, with a choice of fonts. The widget follows the style.

  • Reminders

    Your Google Calendar reminders as Windows notifications.

  • Works offline

    Shows the last loaded schedule when you have no connection.

  • Private by design

    No ads, no tracking, no servers of ours. Your data goes only between your PC and Google.

How Calm Calendar uses your Google data

Calm Calendar signs in with your Google account and asks for two calendar permissions:

  • See the list of your calendars to show your calendars with their names and colors and let you hide or show each one.
  • View and edit events on your calendars to show your events and to create, edit, move and delete events when you do so in the app, and to show reminder notifications.

Sign-in also shares your Google account email address, so the app can show which account you are signed in with.

Everything runs on your computer. The app talks directly to Google; we never receive, store or share your data. You can sign out or remove access at any time.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Calm Calendar is free and has no ads.

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 10 version 1809 or later, and Windows 11.

Does it work with Outlook?

Partly. Calm Calendar is made for Google Calendar, but you can add an Outlook calendar, or any other, by its .ics link in Settings → Calendar subscriptions. Such calendars are read only.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Russian, Ukrainian, Belarusian, Chinese, Japanese, Hindi, Bengali and Arabic. The app follows the Windows language, or you can pick one in Settings → Language.

Can I use more than one Google account?

Yes. Add as many accounts as you need; their calendars are shown together, and a meeting that clashes with another account's event is marked.

How do I remove my data?

Sign out in the app: this revokes access and deletes the saved data from your computer. You can also remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
